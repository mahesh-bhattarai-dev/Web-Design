Flavor · Sharing · Comfort

Food & Flavors

Food is an everyday expression of hospitality, regional identity and celebration.

Dal Bhat

A familiar everyday meal of rice, lentil soup and sides that vary by household and region.

Momo

Steamed or fried dumplings enjoyed with flavorful dipping sauces.

Sel Roti

A ring-shaped rice-flour bread often prepared for festivals and family occasions.

Chiya & Street Snacks

Chiya, samosa, pani puri and chatpate make an inviting break with friends.

Food brings people together

Recipes differ across families and regions. Sharing a meal or offering tea can be as meaningful as the food itself.
