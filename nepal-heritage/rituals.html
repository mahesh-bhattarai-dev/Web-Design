Values & Rituals

Small gestures and shared customs help express respect, belonging and care.

Greetings & Respect

“Namaste” is a widely recognized greeting, often accompanied by palms together. Language and etiquette vary by context.

Tika & Blessings

In some celebrations, tika and blessings symbolize affection, good wishes and intergenerational connection.

Family & Community

Family networks, neighbors and community gatherings can provide practical help and a sense of belonging.

Hospitality & Kindness

Offering food, tea or a welcoming seat is one way people show care for guests and visitors.

Nepal is culturally, linguistically and religiously diverse. Traditions are not identical for every person or community.
