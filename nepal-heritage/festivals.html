Festivals & Celebrations

Celebrations connect generations through family gatherings, blessings, music, lights and shared traditions.

Dashain

A major family festival associated with blessings, tika, jamara and time spent with loved ones.

Tihar

Known for rows of lights, colorful decorations, and honoring crows, dogs, cows and family bonds.

Holi

A vibrant celebration of colors, springtime and community.

Teej

A festival observed by many Hindu women with songs, gathering, devotion and traditional dress.

Festivals and customs vary across Nepal's communities and regions; these are just a few examples.
