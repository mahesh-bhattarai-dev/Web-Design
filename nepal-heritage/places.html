01 / Mountains

The Himalayas

Towering peaks, mountain villages, and trails through extraordinary landscapes.

Everest & Khumbu

Iconic mountain views and Sherpa heritage.

The region is closely connected with Sherpa communities, mountain livelihoods, and routes used by trekkers and climbers. Visitors should plan carefully for altitude, weather, permits, and local guidance.

Annapurna Region

Mountain scenery, villages, and varied trails.

The region includes a range of landscapes and communities, from lower foothills to high mountain trails. Routes vary in difficulty, so the best itinerary depends on time, season, and fitness.

Langtang

Alpine valleys and Himalayan culture.

Langtang offers mountain scenery and villages with distinctive local traditions. Trekking conditions can change with weather and season, so local advice is valuable.

Kanchenjunga Region

Remote landscapes in eastern Nepal.

This remote eastern region is known for dramatic peaks and less-traveled routes. Trips often require additional planning, permits, and respect for local conservation guidance.

02 / History

Heritage Cities

Historic squares, traditional architecture, old streets, and living craftsmanship.

Kathmandu

Historic courtyards, temples, and city life.

The valley blends historic temples, courtyards, markets, and contemporary city life. Exploring respectfully means following site rules and being mindful at active places of worship.

Bhaktapur

Traditional brickwork, pottery, and historic squares.

Visitors can explore traditional architecture, pottery, woodcarving, and public squares. Local craft traditions remain part of the city's living character.

Patan

Art, metalwork, and beautiful Newar architecture.

Patan is known for its artistic heritage, metalwork, and Newar architecture. Its courtyards and monuments reward a slower visit and attention to local customs.

Bandipur

A hilltop town with preserved traditional streets.

Bandipur is known for preserved streets, hilltop views, and traditional Newari-style architecture. It offers a quieter look at historic hill-town life.

Gorkha & Tansen

Historic landmarks and distinctive hill-town character.

These towns connect visitors with Nepal's history, architecture, and hill-town traditions. Each has its own landmarks and local character worth exploring.

03 / Outdoors

Lakes & Nature

Peaceful lakes, forests, wildlife, and landscapes beyond the busy cities.

Phewa Lake, Pokhara

Lake reflections, mountain views, and boat rides.

The lakeside is a popular place for relaxed walks and boat rides, with mountain views when the weather is clear. Conditions and visibility vary by season.

Rara Lake

A remote lake surrounded by forested hills.

Rara is a remote destination valued for its lake, forested surroundings, and peaceful landscape. Travel can take time, so visitors should plan transport and supplies in advance.

Shey Phoksundo

A striking turquoise lake in Dolpo.

The lake is known for striking turquoise water and the distinctive landscapes of Dolpo. Its remote setting makes careful planning and respect for local communities especially important.

Chitwan National Park

Wildlife, grasslands, and subtropical forests.

The park protects important wildlife habitats and offers guided nature experiences. Visitors should choose responsible operators and follow park rules around wildlife.

Gosaikunda & Khaptad

Highland lakes, open meadows, and natural scenery.

These areas offer highland lakes, open meadows, and natural scenery. Conditions can be seasonal, and visitors should check local access and weather information.

Ilam & Koshi Tappu

Tea gardens and important wetland habitats.

Ilam is known for tea-growing landscapes, while Koshi Tappu is important for wetland wildlife and birdlife. These destinations show different sides of Nepal's natural diversity.

04 / Culture

Sacred & Cultural Places

Pilgrimage sites, sacred architecture, and places important to diverse communities.

Lumbini

The birthplace of Siddhartha Gautama, the Buddha.

Lumbini is an important pilgrimage destination associated with the life of the Buddha. Visitors can explore monasteries and sacred sites while observing the quiet and respectful atmosphere.

Pashupatinath

An important Hindu temple complex in Kathmandu.

This major Hindu temple complex is an important place of worship and pilgrimage. Some areas have access restrictions, and visitors should follow posted guidance and local customs.

Boudhanath

A major Buddhist stupa and pilgrimage destination.

The stupa is a significant Buddhist pilgrimage site where visitors may see people walking clockwise around the monument. Move respectfully and follow guidance around religious activity.

Swayambhunath

A hilltop sacred complex overlooking Kathmandu.

This hilltop complex is important to both Buddhist and Hindu traditions and offers broad views over Kathmandu. The site includes stairs and active places of worship.

Janakpur

A major centre of Mithila culture and Hindu tradition.

Janakpur is an important centre of Mithila culture and is associated with the Ramayana tradition. Its art, festivals, and local customs offer insight into the region's cultural identity.

Muktinath

A revered pilgrimage site in the Mustang region.

Muktinath is a revered pilgrimage destination in Mustang, important to Hindu and Buddhist traditions. Its high-altitude setting means visitors should consider acclimatization and changing weather.
