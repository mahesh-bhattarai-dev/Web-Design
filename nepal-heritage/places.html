Discover Nepal

A Country of Many Journeys

From Himalayan landscapes to historic cities, discover the places that make Nepal special.

Explore by category

Where would you like to go?

Choose a region, landscape or cultural destination to discover a few places worth knowing. These short introductions are a starting point for further exploration.

Mountains & Himalayan Regions

High peaks, alpine valleys, mountain communities and trails define many of Nepal's best-known journeys.

Everest & Khumbu

Iconic mountain views and Sherpa heritage. The region is known for high-altitude trekking, mountain villages and deep connections to the Himalayas.

Annapurna Region

Mountain scenery, villages and varied trails. Landscapes shift from subtropical valleys to dramatic high mountain passes.

Langtang

Alpine valleys and Himalayan culture, with mountain views and communities shaped by their highland environment.

Kanchenjunga Region

Remote landscapes in eastern Nepal, with challenging routes, rich biodiversity and expansive mountain scenery.

Historic Towns & Architecture

Courtyards, temples, traditional streets and skilled craft traditions make Nepal's towns cultural destinations in their own right.

Kathmandu

Historic courtyards, temples and city life meet in a busy capital with layers of history, art, markets and everyday culture.

Bhaktapur

Traditional brickwork, pottery and historic squares offer a close look at Newar architecture and craft traditions.

Patan

Art, metalwork and beautiful Newar architecture are part of Patan's long-standing identity as a centre of craftsmanship.

Bandipur

A hilltop town known for preserved traditional streets, historic buildings and wide views across the hills.

Gorkha & Tansen

Historic landmarks and distinctive hill-town character connect these destinations with Nepal's history, local crafts and regional life.

Lakes, Wildlife & Nature

From peaceful lakeshores to protected forests and remote highland landscapes, Nepal's natural places are remarkably varied.

Phewa Lake, Pokhara

Lake reflections, mountain views and boat rides make Phewa Lake a popular place to slow down and enjoy the scenery.

Rara Lake

A remote lake surrounded by forested hills, known for its clear water and quiet setting in northwestern Nepal.

Shey Phoksundo

A striking turquoise lake in Dolpo, surrounded by rugged terrain and landscapes with distinctive natural and cultural character.

Chitwan National Park

Wildlife, grasslands and subtropical forests make Chitwan an important destination for nature and conservation experiences.

Gosaikunda & Khaptad

Highland lakes, open meadows and natural scenery offer very different experiences across Nepal's mountain and hill regions.

Ilam & Koshi Tappu

Ilam is known for tea gardens and green hills; Koshi Tappu is an important wetland habitat for birds and other wildlife.

Sacred & Pilgrimage Places

These places hold religious, historical and cultural significance for many people in Nepal and beyond.

Lumbini

The birthplace of Siddhartha Gautama, the Buddha, and an important pilgrimage destination with monasteries and sacred sites.

Pashupatinath

An important Hindu temple complex in Kathmandu, especially significant for devotees of Lord Shiva and for its riverside rituals.

Boudhanath

A major Buddhist stupa and pilgrimage destination, surrounded by monasteries, shops and a lively community of worshippers and visitors.

Swayambhunath

A hilltop sacred complex overlooking Kathmandu, known for its stupa, prayer flags, religious art and city views.

Janakpur

A major centre of Mithila culture and Hindu tradition, closely associated with the story of Sita and the Ramayana.

Muktinath

A revered pilgrimage site in the Mustang region, important to both Hindu and Buddhist traditions.
