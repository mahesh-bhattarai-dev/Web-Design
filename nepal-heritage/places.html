Places & Heritage

Nepal's heritage stretches from Himalayan landscapes to ancient cities, sacred sites and living neighborhoods.

The Himalayas

Mountain scenery and trails draw visitors from around the world, while remaining part of local life and identity.

Historic Squares

Places such as Bhaktapur Durbar Square preserve architecture, craftsmanship and public spaces.

Lumbini

A significant pilgrimage destination recognized for its connection to the life of Siddhartha Gautama, the Buddha.

Living Cities

Markets, temples, courtyards and neighborhoods show how heritage continues to evolve.

Plan a respectful visit

Learn local customs, follow site guidance, ask before photographing people, and help protect historic places.
