A tradition to discover

Tika: A blessing, a bond, a memory.

During Dashain, many Nepali families gather to receive Tika and blessings from elders. Along with jamara, these moments can represent affection, good wishes, and connections between generations.

Practices and meanings can vary between communities and families, making each tradition worth learning about.
